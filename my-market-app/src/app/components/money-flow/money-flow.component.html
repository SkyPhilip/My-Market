Money Flow

Inferred sector rotation from advancing/declining volume, breadth, and relative strength. Not literal institutional order flow.

What is a Flow Score?

A composite indicator of inferred sector rotation, not actual money moving in or out. Higher scores suggest stronger participation and relative performance; negative scores suggest weaker rotation. Zero means the weighted signals balance, not that no trading occurred. It is not a percentage or a buy/sell signal.

Flow Score = Relative Strength + ((Breadth − 50) / 50 × 2) + (Net Volume Ratio × 3)

Relative Strength
Simple average constituent percentage change minus SPY percentage change, in percentage points.
Breadth
Percentage of tracked constituents with a non-negative change (unchanged stocks count as advancing).
Net Volume Ratio
(Advancing volume − declining volume) / total volume. Each stock's full daily volume goes to its advancing or declining side; this is not measured buying versus selling volume.

Rankings compare sectors by score, not by cash inflow. Even the top-ranked sector can have a negative score. Volume Thrust is shown separately and is not part of the score.
